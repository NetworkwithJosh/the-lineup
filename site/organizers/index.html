The Lineup

Run a cyber, AI or IT meetup? Get it in front of more people.

The Lineup is a free map of cybersecurity, AI and IT meetups. Listing your group costs nothing and takes about a minute.

What your group gets

Your events show up on your state's page, on the live map, and in the Monday email people get for their state. Attendees can mark that they're going and connect with each other before the event, so newcomers walk in already knowing someone. Every event gets its own page and a share image that looks like an invite on LinkedIn.

How to get listed

On Meetup or Luma? Send us your group link and we follow your calendar automatically, twice a day. New events, changes and cancellations update on their own. Nothing to keep in sync.

Somewhere else? Submit a recurring meetup or a single event and we'll add it after a quick check.

Our rules

We only list events that are open to the public and actually about cyber, AI or IT. We link straight to your page for RSVPs, we don't sell tickets, and we never copy your attendee list. If something about your listing is wrong, use the "Something wrong with this listing?" link on its page and we'll fix it.
