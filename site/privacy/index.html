The Lineup

Privacy

Plain-English version. Last updated November 26, 2026.

What we collect

Browsing The Lineup needs no account. If you make one, we store your email, first and last name, role, experience level, optional company, which events you mark as going, and your connect requests, blocks and reports. We use privacy-friendly analytics (no ad cookies). If you use "Near me", your location stays in your browser and is never sent to us.

Who sees what

Anyone can see how many people are going to an event. Only signed-in people who are also going can see who: first name, last initial, role and experience. Your email and full last name are shared only with someone after you both accept a connect request. Your company is shown only to connections, and only if you turn it on.

Emails

With an account you get sign-in links, and (if you leave them on) an email when someone wants to connect or accepts, plus a Monday email with your home state's events. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe, and you can change these any time on your account page.

Where it lives

Account data is stored with Supabase (our database provider). Emails are sent through Resend (our email provider). We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't share it with advertisers or recruiters.

Your controls

Edit your profile, un-mark going, block people or delete your account any time from your account page. Deleting your account removes your data from our database right away (backups roll off within 30 days).

Age

Accounts are for people 18 and older.

Contact

Questions or data requests: use the report button in your account or the contact form.

The Lineup lists events other people organize and refreshes every day. Check the organizer's page before you go.
