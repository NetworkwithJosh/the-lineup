Run a cybersecurity, AI or IT meetup, or know one that's missing? Send it here. Groups are best: give us the Meetup, Luma or calendar link and every future event shows up on its own, with changes and cancellations kept in sync.

What we list

Free or paid events that are open to the public: chapter meetings (ISACA, ISSA, OWASP, ISC2, CSA), DEF CON groups and CitySec nights, BSides, AI and cloud user groups, career and networking nights. No sales webinars or private events. Every submission is checked by a person before it goes live, usually within a few days.
