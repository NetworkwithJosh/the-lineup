# BRIEF: The Lineup

Interviewed (short form, Nov 15 2026). Answers below are Josh's choices; anything marked AUTHORED is a design decision made from those answers.

## What it is and who it's for
A public site that pulls together cybersecurity and AI networking meetups and conferences that already exist (NY/NJ/PA first, plus US, international and online), so a working professional can see what's on without checking ten sites. It does not organize or accept meetups. Read-only aggregator.

Josh, verbatim: "this is really just focusing on scraping information and coming together of all cybersecurity meetups. But I don't want it to be able to organize meetups. I want to see what meetups are already organized."

## The eight topics
1. Vibe: "Dark + technical". AUTHORED: a night-shift operations board. Off-black, cold slate, one signal-amber accent (rotated away from AI-purple and from cream-and-brass).
2. Journey: AUTHORED (tool grammar): board already loaded at the top, then filters, then the list, then the feed.
3. Energy curve: calm at the top (one number and the slider), busiest in the list, quiet at the feed close.
4. Feeling / one moment: the moment you drag "I'm off at" and the list rearranges itself to only what you can actually make tonight.
5. Signature: "Workday slider". Drag the time you get off work and your commute, and the board shows only events you can reach, with the arrival line per event ("You'd walk in 12 min early").
6. Range: dark, technical, dense rather than airy.
7. World vs scenes: neither. Live surface grammar: no world, the product is the page.
8. Assets: none. "No generated assets". No kie.ai spend.

## Grammar
Live surface (2.3). The page is the working tool on real scraped data. The other seven lost because: filmic, editorial, poster, gallery, split, cutlist and continuous world are all marketing or story shapes; the honest pitch here is "use it", and live surface is the only grammar whose hero is the product in a state and whose close is an input.

## Feeling curve
- Board header: orientation. Caused by one plain count of what's coming up and the date.
- Workday slider (PEAK): relief / "oh, I can actually go to that". Caused by the list re-sorting under your thumb with arrival lines.
- Filters + list: browsing curiosity. Caused by dense, labelled rows with real dates, places and costs.
- Feed close: commitment, quiet. Caused by one field-like row: the calendar feed URL with a copy button.

## The peak
"I dragged it to 6pm and it showed me the three meetups I can still make tonight." Lives in the slider panel at the top of the board.

## Tell-someone sentence
It's the site where you tell it when you get off work and it shows you which cyber and AI meetups you can still make.

## Authored silence
None. The page is short (a working surface, not a cinematic page).
